# Transformed content

BuddyAR-adapted versions of approved pilot assets (shortened, reformatted, re-recorded,
translated, etc.) live here — kept **separate** from the untouched sources in `../pilot/`.

- Record every transformation in the source asset's `changes_made` field in the manifest.
- A derivative of a **CC BY-SA** asset must itself remain CC BY-SA — keep such derivatives
  clearly labelled and do not merge them into proprietary BuddyAR content.
- Nothing should be transformed until its source asset is `approved-for-pilot`.
